1. EXECUTIVE EXPERIENCE PHILOSOPHY & INTERACTION ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================

Why Scrollytelling for Ayush Tandon?
You are not just a frontend developer or a standard junior coder; you are a 
Full-Stack AI Engineer who architects zero-trust LLM security gateways, autonomous 
PR review intelligence agents, enterprise incubator CRMs, and high-concurrency backends.
A standard portfolio with navbar clicks treats projects like isolated static cards. 
A scrollytelling portfolio treats your career as a single unified system:
- Scrolling down does NOT just shift pixels; it powers up systems.
- Every scroll input controls time, depth, and visibility.
- Mask reveals and sticky pins create the illusion of a living, breathing software 
  application that unfolds organically.

Key Animation Mechanics Employed:
1. Sticky Pinning: The viewport locks in place while internal layers translate, rotate, 
   or cross-fade over a defined scroll distance (e.g., 200vh-300vh).
2. Mask Reveals: Utilizing CSS `clip-path: circle(r at x y)` or `polygon()` driven by 
   scroll percentage to transition from dark/terminal themes into architectural canvases.
3. Scrubbed Kinetic Typography: Paragraphs start dim/grayed out and light up word-by-word 
   or letter-by-letter as the user scrolls through the section.
4. Depth & Parallax: Background particles, grid distortion, and foreground UI cards 
   move at contrasting velocities, creating a tactile three-dimensional workspace.
